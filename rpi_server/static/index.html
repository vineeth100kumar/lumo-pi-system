Apple Shortcuts for LUMO

Send photos directly from your iPhone/iPad Photos app in 1 tap, or automate sync when charging at night.
Your LUMO Shortcut Ingestion URL Port 8081 • No SSL Error
http://...:8081/api/memories/shortcut
⚡ Uses plain HTTP on port 8081: Completely eliminates iOS "invalid certificate" warnings!
Send one or multiple photos at once from the iOS Photos app via Share Sheet:
1
Open Shortcuts app on iPhone > Tap + > Name it Send to LUMO.
2
Tap the (i) Details icon at bottom > Turn ON Show in Share Sheet > Select Images.
3
Add Action: Repeat with Each > Input: Shortcut Input.
4
Inside loop, Add Action: Convert Image > input Repeat Item to JPEG.
5
Inside loop, Add Action: Get Contents of URL:
  • URL: Paste the copied URL above
  • Method: POST
  • Request Body: File > select Converted Image
6
Outside loop, Add Action: Show Notification > "✨ Synced photos to LUMO Frame!"
🎉 Multiple photos supported! In the Photos app, tap Select, pick 1, 5, or 20 photos, tap Share > Send to LUMO. They will all beam to LUMO in batch!
Automatically sync ALL favorite photos taken today every night when charging:
1
Open Shortcuts app > Tap Automation tab at the bottom > Tap +.
2
Choose Charger > When iPhone is Connected to Power (or At 10:00 PM) > Select Run Immediately.
3
Add Action: Find Photos > Filter where Is a Favorite is Yes & Date Taken is Today (or in the last 1 day).
4
Add Action: Repeat with Each > Input: Photos.
5
Inside loop, Add Action: Convert Image > input Repeat Item to JPEG.
6
Inside loop, Add Action: Get Contents of URL > POST to shortcut URL above (Request Body: File > Converted Image).
🌙 Zero-Click Multi-Photo Sync: When you dock your phone at night, every single favorite photo from your day is automatically synced to your desk frame!
A standalone 1-tap Home Screen button to select multiple photos and send immediately:
1
Open Shortcuts app > Tap + > Name it Beam Photos to LUMO.
2
Add Action: Select Photos > Tap the arrow (>) > Turn ON Select Multiple.
3
Add Action: Repeat with Each > Input: Photos.
4
Inside loop: Convert Image (Repeat Item to JPEG) > Get Contents of URL (POST File).
5
Tap the share icon at bottom > Add to Home Screen (or assign to Action Button).
📲 Home Screen Widget: Tap the icon anytime, pick 10 photos from your gallery, and they will all stream to LUMO!
Single or batch multi-photo upload via cURL:
Single Photo:
curl -X POST -F "file=@photo.jpg" http://...:8081/api/memories/shortcut
Multiple Photos in 1 Batch:
curl -X POST -F "files=@pic1.jpg" -F "files=@pic2.jpg" -F "files=@pic3.jpg" http://...:8081/api/memories/shortcut
